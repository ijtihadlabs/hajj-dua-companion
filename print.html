La ilaha illal-lahu wahdahu la shareeka lah, lahul-mulku walahul-hamd, wahuwa AAala kulli shay-in qadeer, ayiboona ta-iboon, AAabidoon, lirabbina hamidoon, sadaqal-lahu waAAdah, wanasara AAabdah, wahazamal-ahzaba wahdah

None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, alone, without partner. To Him belongs all sovereignty and praise, and He is over all things omnipotent. We return, repent, worship and praise our Lord. Allah fulfilled His promise, aided His Servant, and single-handedly defeated the allies

(Ibn AAumar reported that the Messenger of Allah on return from a battle or from performing the pilgrimage would say at every high point: (Allah is the greatest, Allah is the greatest, Allah is the greatest.) and then he would say: (None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, alone, without partner. To Him belongs all sovereignty and praise, and He is over all things omnipotent. We return, repent, worship and praise our Lord. Allah fulfilled His promise, aided His Servant, and single-handedly defeated the allies.))

Forgiveness

Qur’an · Qur’an · Baqarah # 127, 128

Qur’an · Qur’an · Baqarah # 285, 286

We have heard and obeyed. We seek Your forgiveness, O Allah, and to You is our return. O Allah do not punish us for the sins we committed by mistake or in error. Do not burden us with such heavy duties as had been placed on the people before us. O Allah, do not burden us with that which is beyond our ability. Overlook our sins, Forgive us and have mercy on us. You are our Master so help us against the disbelievers
